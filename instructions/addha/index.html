ADDHA

One vector lands in every active row of the ZA tile.

Types: 32-bit integer — the z0.s lanes and the 4 × 4 za0.s tile are all 32-bit containers. ADDHA's .s class is the integer form (its .d twin is the 64-bit integer variant); ADDHA has no floating-point form. Drawn at SVL = 128 bits; at SVL = 512 the tile is 16 × 16 and one vector still lands in every row.

Reading the picture

  1. Broadcast into every row

    For each active pair, ZA[i, j] gains z0.s[j]: the vector is added to every horizontal slice that the row predicate allows. One instruction, a whole-tile update.

  2. Two predicates, like FMOPA

    p0/m governs rows and p1/m governs columns — the same shape as FMOPA. There the product depends on both i and j; here only the column picks the value, and it is the same in every row.

  3. ADDVA is the twin

    ADDVA is the vertical twin: ZA[i, j] gains z0.s[i], so the vector runs down every active column instead of along every row. Play ADDVA
