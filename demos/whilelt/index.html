Reading the picture

  1. Where predicates come from

    Every SVE loop starts like this: compare two scalars and get one bit per lane. The ld1w and fmla pages let you click those bits by hand — whilelt is the instruction that sets them in hardware.

  2. Length-agnostic

    The same instruction works at any streaming vector length. Drawn here at 128 bits it produces four bits; on hardware with a 2048-bit vector it would produce sixty-four — and the loop code would not change.

  3. Feeding the next instruction

    This p0 is the p0/z of ld1w and the p0/m of fmla: whilelt decides which lanes those instructions touch. Move x1 with the stepper and watch the active window slide.
