Reading the picture

  1. Even and odd

    uzp1 takes elements 0 and 2 of each source — the dimmed odd lanes never move. uzp2 takes 1 and 3 instead; together they split one interleaved stream into two.

  2. Why deinterleave

    Stereo audio, colour planes, interleaved captures: memory often arrives interleaved, while SIMD wants contiguous lanes of one kind. uzp is that bridge — and zip1 interleaves back.

  3. No predicate here

    uzp moves whole vectors, so there is no p0. Every element is data being shuffled, not work being masked — a different animal from every other page in this lab.
