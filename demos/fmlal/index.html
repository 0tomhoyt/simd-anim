FMLAL

Half-precision operands, single-precision tile: a widening multiply-add with an indexed vector.

Types: z0.h, z1.h — 16-bit half floats; the tile accumulates 32-bit singles, so every product widens. Drawn at SVL = 128 bits: 8 half operands and a 16-slice ZA.S array; at SVL = 512: 32 halfs, 64 slices, the index picking within each 128-bit segment.

Reading the picture

  1. Narrow in, wide out

    z0.h and z1.h hold 16-bit halfs; the tile accumulates 32-bit singles. Every product is widened before it lands — the classic mixed-precision trick for more throughput per byte.

  2. One indexed operand

    The multiplier is a single half picked from z1.h by the [index] immediate. Switch the index above and watch the whole tile pair rescale — kernels use this to walk a weight column without reloading.

  3. Two slices at once

    W11 plus the offset 4:5 selects two adjacent ZA slices: even halfs of z0.h feed slice 4, odd halfs feed slice 5, in element order. There is no predicate — every element lands.
