Reading the picture

  1. Contiguous addressing

    Element i loads from x0 + 4 × i: one word per .s lane, walking memory in order. Four active lanes cover exactly sixteen bytes.

  2. Zeroing with /z

    Loads take /z, not /m: an inactive lane is written with 0 instead of keeping its old value. Click a predicate bit and watch that lane reset to zero. Compare with FMLA and FMOPA, which merge.

  3. No stray reads

    Predicated-off elements cause no memory access at all. The dimmed words at [x0,#16] and [x0,#20] are never touched, no matter what they hold.
