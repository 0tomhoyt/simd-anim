Reading the picture

  1. Vertical to horizontal

    FMLA and FMOPA work along lanes; addv collapses the lanes into one scalar. A reduction is the vector answer to “what is the total?”.

  2. The predicate picks the lanes

    p0/m chooses which lanes join the sum — click a bit and that value drops out. The scalar d0 is always fully written; there is nothing to merge into.

  3. A family of reductions

    smaxv and sminv share this exact shape with maximum and minimum in place of the sum; faddv even starts from an existing scalar instead of zero.
