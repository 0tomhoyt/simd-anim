Linear algebra self-check

Three questions on matrix multiply and the dot product. Nothing here is required to start the path.

  1. A is M×K and B is K×N. What shape is C = AB?

    Show answer

    M×N.

  2. What is one entry C[i, j] of that product?

    Show answer

    The dot product of row i of A and column j of B.

  3. FMOPA writes an outer product into a tile. How is that different from a dot product?

    Show answer

    An outer product fills a tile: every element of one vector times every element of the other. A dot product collapses one pair of vectors to a single number.
