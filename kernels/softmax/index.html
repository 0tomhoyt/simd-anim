Softmax

Subtract the max, then exp, then divide. The largest exp is 1. A naive exp can overflow while this one stays finite.

Reading the picture

  1. The max is the whole trick

    Subtracting the max shifts the peak to 0. exp(0) is 1, and every other exp is smaller. The ratios do not change, so the weights do not change.

  2. Naive exp overflows

    exp(1000) is Inf. The overflow preset shows those values going Inf, while the shifted ones stay between −3 and 0 and the weights still sum to 1.
