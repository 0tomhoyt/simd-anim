Reading the picture

  1. Scores are dots

    Each step dots the chosen query with one key and writes that score. Token 2 matches key 2, so that score is the largest. Pick another query and the winning key moves.

  2. Softmax is not a GEMM

    The weights are the scores after a stable softmax. They sum to 1, and the brightest V row is the key the query matched. The exp and the max subtraction live on their own page. Open the softmax page

  3. Two GEMMs, softmax between them

    QKᵀ writes the scores and the mix with V writes the output. Both are the same shape as the microkernel, and both want their panels packed first. Open the pack page Open the microkernel
